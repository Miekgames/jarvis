JARVIS v1.2.0: chat with JARVIS on Discord.

## Install

Download **JARVIS-v1.2.0-windows.zip** below, unzip it, and double-click **JARVIS.bat**. You need Windows 10 or 11 and Python 3.9 or newer; Node.js isn't needed. Upgrading? Unzip in place of the old folder, keeping your `.env` and `data` folder; the first start installs the one new package (discord.py).

## New: JARVIS on Discord

Message your own Discord bot and the same JARVIS answers: the same tools, permissions and audit log as the screen.

1. In the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications), choose **New Application**, then **Bot**, then **Reset Token**, and copy the token.
2. In JARVIS, turn on **Chat through Discord** in Permissions, open the **Discord** panel and paste the token. It's sealed on your PC with Windows' own encryption and never shown again.
3. Say **"pair Discord"** (or press Pair). DM your bot the code JARVIS shows. Discord only lets you message a bot you share a server with, so the panel gives you a link to add it to one of yours.

How it stays safe:
- It's a bot account, marked BOT by Discord, never your own account.
- Only paired accounts get JARVIS. Anyone else who messages the bot gets one polite line, at most once an hour, and nothing runs.
- Anything that needs your OK is asked in the chat, and only your "yes" approves it.
- Servers are off by default, since replies there are visible to everyone in the channel. Say "answer me in Discord servers too" to allow @mentions.
- "Turn off Discord", "unpair Discord", or switching the permission off stop it at once.

Also: **"send my notifications to Discord"** sends reminders and finished research to your DMs. It's off until you ask, because it sends their text through Discord. Looked-up answers come with their sources as links.
