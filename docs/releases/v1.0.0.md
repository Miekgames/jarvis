The first downloadable release of JARVIS: a personal AI command centre that runs on your own computer, with the AI of your choice (your own key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Together or any OpenAI-compatible service, or fully local with Ollama).

## Install

1. Download **JARVIS-v1.0.0-windows.zip** below and unzip it anywhere.
2. Double-click **JARVIS.bat**.

You need **Windows 10 or 11** and **Python 3.9 or newer** ([python.org](https://www.python.org/downloads/); tick "Add python.exe to PATH"). If Python is missing, JARVIS.bat tells you how to install it. Node.js is **not** needed: the interface comes already built.

The first run takes a few minutes while it installs its Python packages into a `.venv` folder next to it. After that it starts straight away and opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:8000. The setup screen then asks which AI to use and for its key. Your key stays on your computer.

## What's in it

- **Just say it:** settings, panels, reminders, research and stopping work all happen by asking ("turn on the wake word", "switch to the Ember theme", "remind me at 7 to call Dad", "stop the research"), with no menus.
- **Today's facts, looked up and read:** "who's projected to win tonight's game?", "what's the score?", "latest news on…". JARVIS reads the day's articles first and answers with the source, instead of guessing or pointing you elsewhere.
- **Long research:** 15 to 20 minutes of searching and reading, then a four-paragraph report with sources and what's next. The bottom-left corner of the screen fills as a progress bar while it runs.
- **Skill colours:** each skill has its own colour, and the screen fades to it while the skill is in use (orange for research), then back to blue.
- **Reminders and scheduled notifications**, also shown as Windows notifications.
- **Email**, **countdowns**, **everyday lookups** (units, currency, world clock, Wikipedia, definitions), **your documents** with cited answers, **memory**, **skills**, **MCP plug-ins**, **While away** mode, **tasks, schedules and automations**, **system monitoring**, and **developer tools**.

Every capability has its own permission switch, sensitive actions ask first, and everything JARVIS does is logged.
