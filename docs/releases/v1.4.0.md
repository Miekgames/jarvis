JARVIS v1.4.0: checklists, and scrolling back through earlier messages.

## Install

Download **JARVIS-v1.4.0-windows.zip** below, unzip it, and double-click **JARVIS.bat**. You need Windows 10 or 11 and Python 3.9 or newer; Node.js isn't needed. Upgrading? Unzip in place of the old folder, keeping your `.env` and `data` folder. Your database is upgraded on first start.

## New

- **Checklists.** A grocery list, a workout, a packing list: anything to tick off.
  - Say "make a grocery list: eggs, milk and bread", "add butter to my grocery list", "check off eggs" (or "uncheck eggs"), "what's on my grocery list?", "reset my workout list" or "clear the checked items from my grocery list".
  - "Show me my checklists" opens a menu of them. Pick one, then tap items to check and uncheck, add or remove things, uncheck everything or clear the checked ones.
  - Whatever you say shows in the open panel straight away. Deleting a whole list asks first.
  - It works on Discord too, where the replies list the items.
- **Scroll back.** Earlier messages are on screen when JARVIS opens, on the computer or the phone, and scrolling up loads older ones, back through earlier days. While you're reading back, nothing fades and the newest messages don't pull you down. "↓ latest" takes you back. A conversation from the last few hours carries on where it left off.
- `/help checklists` lists everything you can say.
