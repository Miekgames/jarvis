JARVIS v1.5.0: Discord calls and voice messages, emojis, and a clean screen that remembers.

## Install

Download **JARVIS-v1.5.0-windows.zip** below, unzip it, and double-click **JARVIS.bat**. You need Windows 10 or 11 and Python 3.9 or newer; Node.js isn't needed. Upgrading? Unzip in place of the old folder, keeping your `.env` and `data` folder.

## New

- **Discord voice messages.** Hold the mic in your bot's DM and ask out loud. It's transcribed on your PC (nothing is sent anywhere else) and answered like anything typed, with what it heard shown above the answer. This is handy on a phone, where browsers block the microphone on plain `http://` addresses.
- **Discord calls (optional).** Say "call me on Discord", optionally with a message ("…and tell me the oven's done"):
  - The bot DMs you a link to a voice channel. When you join, it joins and speaks in JARVIS's voice.
  - While you're on the call, what you type to the bot is answered out loud too.
  - "Hang up" ends it, or just leave. A call nobody joins ends after 5 minutes as a missed call.
  - "Call me for reminders" makes reminders ring this way.
  - The bot joins deafened, because it doesn't listen.
  - Calls are an optional extra: run `.venv\Scripts\python -m pip install -r requirements-voice.txt` in the JARVIS folder, then restart. The bot needs Connect and Speak permission in a voice channel. Like everything Discord, it's off until you set up your own bot, and it uses your own token.
- **Emojis.** JARVIS uses an emoji now and then where it adds meaning (✅ 🛒 📅). They're never read aloud. "Stop using emojis" or "use emojis" switches them.
- **A clean screen that remembers.** Reloading or reopening JARVIS starts with an empty screen, but the conversation carries on, so JARVIS still knows what you just said. "Earlier messages" brings the history back.

## Note for Discord calls

Discord now encrypts voice end to end. JARVIS waits until the encryption is ready before speaking, and the voice extra pins `davey` below 0.2, because with 0.2.0 the bot's audio is sent but can't be heard.
