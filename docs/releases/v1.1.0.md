JARVIS v1.1.0: better on your phone, answers you can check, and "undo that".

## Install

Download **JARVIS-v1.1.0-windows.zip** below, unzip it, and double-click **JARVIS.bat**. You need Windows 10 or 11 and Python 3.9 or newer; Node.js isn't needed. Already on v1.0.0? Unzip this one in place of the old folder, keeping your `.env` and `data` folder.

## New

- **Add it to your home screen.** Open JARVIS in your phone's browser and use Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or Install app (Android). It opens full screen with its own icon, without the browser's bars.
- **Fits a phone.** On a phone-sized screen the clock moves beside the menu with a short date, so it no longer runs into "STANDING BY", and replies stay readable where they cross the core's rings. The command box keeps clear of the iPhone's home bar.
- **Sources you can tap.** When JARVIS looks something up ("who's projected to win tonight's game?"), the articles it read are listed under the answer as links.
- **"Undo that."** Say "undo that" or "change it back" after changing a setting or the theme by asking, and it goes back the way it was.

## Fixed

- After an update, a phone could keep showing the old version until the browser decided to check again. The page is now always checked fresh, while the rest is cached for speed.
- The built interface is now only served from its own folder: a crafted address can no longer reach other files beside it.
- Looked-up answers no longer mention today's date for no reason.
