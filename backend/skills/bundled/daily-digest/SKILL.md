# Daily digest

Gather, in this order, and skip any that are unavailable without fuss:

1. Today's schedule -- `calendar_view` for today.
2. Weather -- `weather`.
3. Due and overdue items -- `deadline_check`.
4. Anything JARVIS was working on -- `day_summary`.

Then write the digest as something to read in under a minute:

- **Good morning** line with the date.
- **Today** -- events in time order, with gaps worth using.
- **Weather** -- one line, plus what to wear or bring if it matters.
- **Don't forget** -- deadlines and reminders.
- **Suggested focus** -- the single most important thing, and why.

Be brief and specific. No motivational filler.
