# Researching a topic properly

The request is the topic, in the owner's words.

1. Call `research_topic` with the topic exactly as the owner put it.
2. Tell the owner in one sentence that the research has started, roughly how long it
   takes, and that the report will appear here and as a notification.
3. Do not answer the question yourself in the meantime, and do not guess at what the
   research will find.
