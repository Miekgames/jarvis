# Drafting an email

1. Work out who it is to, what it is about, and what outcome the owner wants. If
   they pasted an email they are replying to, read it for every question it asks.
2. Check memory for how the owner writes and anything known about the recipient
   (`recall` with the recipient's name).
3. Write the draft:
   - a greeting that fits how well they know the person;
   - one short paragraph per point, answering every question asked;
   - a clear next step or ask;
   - a sign-off in the owner's usual style.
4. Keep it shorter than feels necessary. Plain words, no filler, no "I hope this
   email finds you well".
5. Show the draft and offer one alternative tone (warmer or more formal).

Never send anything. Drafting is the whole job; the owner sends it.
